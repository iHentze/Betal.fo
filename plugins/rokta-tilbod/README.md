# Røkta tilboð

Betal turns a product on sale into a post the shop can put on Facebook and Instagram.

The shop owner does not open Canva. They pick the offer, look at three posters, and copy a Faroese caption that already has a Betal payment link.

Open `prototype/index.html` in a browser to click through the three screens: Tilboð, Plakat, Opslag.

## What is fixed

- The photo is theirs. The price is real text, not drawn by an image model.
- Sizes are 4:5, 1:1, and 9:16.
- The first version is a name, two prices, an end date, and a photo. No shop connection yet.
- Posting into Meta waits. The owner copies the text and downloads the images.

The decisions for anyone building this are in `skills/rokta-tilbod/SKILL.md`.
